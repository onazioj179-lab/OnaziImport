About this website

Onazi’s Import supplies wholesale and retail goods from FCT Abuja, Nigeria. This website introduces our collections and provides ways to contact the store. It is not an online checkout, and preparing or sending an enquiry does not itself place an order or take payment.

These terms describe use of the website and the enquiry process. Any specific order details must be agreed directly with the store. Nothing here limits rights or remedies that cannot be excluded under applicable law.

Products and availability

Collection descriptions are examples of the types of products we sell. They are not a live inventory or a guarantee that a particular item, brand, size, colour or model is available. Please confirm the exact product and any specifications important to you before ordering or visiting.

For appliances and gadgets, ask about power requirements, included accessories, instructions and any product-specific warranty. Do not assume a manufacturer warranty applies unless it is confirmed for that item.

Quotes, orders and payment

Contact the team for current retail prices or a wholesale quote. Trade pricing and minimum quantities depend on the product, quantities and available stock. Ask how long a quote remains valid.

Before you commit, confirm the items, quantities, total price, any delivery charges, payment method and handover arrangements directly with the store. No payment is processed on this website. Verify payment instructions with the store through the contact details on this website and keep your receipt or order confirmation.

If you need to change or cancel an agreed order, contact the team promptly to discuss its status and applicable rights.

Collection and delivery

Store collection is available by arrangement. The listed location is behind Sharia Court, at the Block Industry, FCT Abuja, Nigeria. Call to confirm directions, opening times and the item’s availability before travelling.

If you require delivery, ask whether it can be arranged for your location. Any delivery fee and estimated timing must be confirmed before the order is agreed. This website does not promise nationwide delivery, free delivery or a fixed delivery time.

Returns and product support

If an item is faulty, unsafe, does not match what was agreed, or another purchase issue arises, contact 0816 404 3196 with your receipt or order details and a description of the problem. Photos or product details may help the team assess the issue and discuss the appropriate next steps.

Rights and remedies under applicable Nigerian consumer law remain available, including appropriate repair, replacement or refund where the law requires. These terms do not impose a blanket “no refunds” rule or remove statutory rights. For a change-of-mind return or a product-specific warranty, ask the store to confirm the applicable arrangements before buying.

For independent consumer information and complaints, visit the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission.
