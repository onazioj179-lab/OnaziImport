What the website uses

This website does not set browser cookies or load analytics, advertising scripts, embedded maps or social media widgets. It uses local storage to remember your response to the privacy notice. Local storage is a browser storage mechanism separate from cookies.

All fonts are hosted with the website. Visiting a policy page does not enable optional tracking. There are currently no optional tracking categories to switch on.

The preference we remember

Storage key: onazi:privacy-choice.

Purpose: remember whether you selected “Necessary only” or “Accept & close”, along with the notice version and an expiry timestamp. It contains no name, contact information, enquiry text or visitor identifier. This preference is not sent to us by the website.

Duration: your choice is recognised for 180 days. After that, the notice appears again on your next page visit. Local storage does not automatically delete expired entries; the old record remains until replaced by a new choice or cleared using your browser settings.

If browser storage is blocked, you can still close the notice, browse the site and prepare an enquiry. In that case, the notice may reappear when you navigate or reload.

Your choice

Both buttons close the notice and remember your selection. Neither button enables analytics or advertising, because those services are not installed. “Accept & close” acknowledges the notice; it does not accept the shopping terms or grant marketing consent.

Use the button below or “Cookie settings” in the footer to reopen the notice. You can also remove the saved record through your browser’s site-data controls. If the website later introduces optional tracking, this policy and the consent controls must be updated before that tracking is enabled.

Links and hosting

Following a WhatsApp or other external link takes you to a service with its own cookies and privacy practices. Those services are not loaded as embedded widgets on this website. GitHub Pages provides the hosting infrastructure; see our privacy policy for hosting information.
